      Questions

      A straightforward place to begin.

      What does FirstNotice do?

      It takes a claim description, identifies the type of claim, extracts key reported details, and returns a concise structured summary for a carrier team's review.

      Does it make claim decisions?

      No. FirstNotice is designed to support intake organization. Claim review, coverage decisions, and professional judgment stay with the carrier team.

      Where does it fit in the workflow?

      At the beginning: after claim information is received and before it moves into review, routing, or the carrier's downstream claim process.
